# INKBORN — The Monster Made of Your Mistakes

Your mistakes become monsters. Your understanding destroys them.

## The Unique Kernel

The student's actual drawing becomes a living boss whose three physical vulnerabilities represent three concepts from their study notes. Wrong answers visibly corrupt the corresponding vulnerability; correct answers shatter it. After a mistake, an evidence-grounded explanation and a distinct follow-up question let the student demonstrate improved understanding. The creature's changing appearance communicates what still needs practice.

## Who It's For

A student revising engineering, mathematics, basic science or another subject expressible through short factual notes and reliable multiple-choice questions, especially someone who finds passive revision hard to sustain. They bring their own notes and turn a small revision session into a focused learning battle.

## The Core Loop

Open the atmospheric title screen, begin or resume a session, paste notes or explicitly select a curated sample, and draw a creature. Watch that recognizable drawing awaken with three named concept weak points. Answer questions, see the matching weak point react, learn from grounded feedback, and try a different question after a mistake. Finish with a clear account of initial correct answers, corrections after feedback and unresolved topics; review the evidence, preserve the session and start another battle.

## Inspiration & Identity

An ancient forbidden illustrated storybook brought to life inside an alchemist's chamber. Hand-drawn ink, weathered parchment, occult geometry, controlled fog and ash, fractured crystals, layered shadows and cinematic lighting. Charcoal and midnight ink support aged ivory text, burgundy danger, restrained violet instability, antique gold success and soft teal detail. Dramatic readable serif titles accompany clean gameplay text.

The creature must feel alive through expressive transform-based animation that preserves arbitrary drawings. Motion, sound, corruption and shattering should communicate learning events. The title, drawing chamber, arena and results must feel like one authored world. No external visual reference or asset source has been selected yet.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

The owner wants to turn an ambitious idea into a complete, polished application using structured AI-assisted development, improve architectural judgment and verification, and create a competitive hackathon entry they are proud to demonstrate. The learning system, game behavior and visual quality all matter; a conventional quiz with a passive monster backdrop would fail the vision. No prize or measured learning gain is promised.

## What "Working" Looks Like

A fresh visitor can understand the premise and complete a local battle without an API key using a clearly labeled sample. They draw a distinctive creature, see that actual drawing awaken, make a mistake that visibly corrupts its matching concept weak point, read an explanation supported by the notes, and answer a different question to shatter that weak point. The session can also finish with unresolved concepts and report them accurately. Results preserve the difference between first-attempt correctness and correctness after feedback.

The same battle engine accepts validated, evidence-linked material genuinely generated from fresh notes through a server-side AI integration when valid credentials are configured. Missing credentials, unsuitable notes, invalid output and provider failures have honest recovery. Live AI verification requires actual provider access; an unrun live check must remain explicitly unverified.

The full local application, persistence, automated checks, production build, documentation and genuine Devpost artifacts must support repeatable demonstrations. Demonstrate at least two meaningfully different sample note sets and drawings. The memorable proof is a previously failed concept visibly changing from corruption to destruction when the student answers its follow-up correctly.

## The POC Boundary

- One complete local web-game journey: title and help/settings, notes, drawing and preview, awakening, battle, results/review, saved-session resume and another battle.
- Three distinct concepts per battle, each consistently attached to its own visible weak point. Initial and distinct follow-up questions, explanations and hints support a finite session with accurate outcomes, including unresolved concepts.
- A real drawing workbench with pen, brush size, eraser, palette, undo/redo, clear, blank prevention and cropped sprite preparation; pointer, mouse and touch support. The recognizable silhouette and colors remain the boss's visual basis.
- Cohesive ink-fantasy art direction, expressive creature states, meaningful audiovisual feedback, sound controls, reduced motion, keyboard focus and usable smaller-screen layouts. Desktop composition is the priority.
- A genuine server-side AI path with structured validation and verifiable note references, plus explicitly labeled curated sample content sharing the same gameplay systems. Secrets stay server-side in environment variables.
- Local session and completed-summary persistence, robust failure recovery, core-logic and browser verification, a working local production launch, technical documentation and license/attribution records. The owner chooses the project license during planning.
- Fast-mode execution after planning approval, with required early firsthand feedback, final learner review and learning wrap-up. Scope, PRD and spec remain separate approval gates.

This is one polished vertical slice, rather than a broad education platform. Its quality and verification requirements exceed the curriculum's usual 2–4-hour exercise; that is a scope tradeoff for owner review, not a promised delivery time. A short demo video and public GitHub repository are required shipping artifacts; deployment is optional. Public publishing requires explicit authorization, and submission writing stays with the owner.

## Later

Proposed extensions beyond the supplied single-session experience: accounts and cross-device cloud sync, long-term practice scheduling, broader content ingestion such as PDF/OCR, additional encounters and campaign progression. None is a commitment for this build.

## Explicitly Cut

- Automatic skeletal rigging of arbitrary doodles: unreliable for free-form silhouettes; use expressive transforms and effects instead, as requested.
- AI control of combat rules or animation timing: deterministic gameplay is needed for coherent, testable outcomes.
- Substituting generic artwork for the player's drawing, unlabeled sample content posing as generated questions, unsupported evidence, or claims of mastery from reading feedback: each contradicts the product's core promise or honest reporting.
- Mandatory deployment and AI-authored submission/feedback/survey text: the requested deliverable is a complete local product, with public actions reviewed separately and human authorship preserved.
